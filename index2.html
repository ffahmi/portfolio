Background

Crafting digital precision with purpose.

I am a Senior UI/UX Designer and Product Strategist currently based in Johor, Malaysia. My journey in design began with a fascination for the intersection of human psychology and digital interfaces, how the placement of a single pixel can influence user trust and behavior.

Over the past decade, I've partnered with venture-backed startups and established tech giants to ship products that balance technical constraints with uncompromising aesthetic standards. I believe that good design is invisible; it should solve complex problems without adding friction to the user's cognitive load.

My approach is deeply rooted in the "developer-adjacent" philosophy. By understanding the underlying architecture of modern frameworks like Tailwind and Laravel, I bridge the gap between creative vision and functional implementation, ensuring that design intent is never lost in translation.
